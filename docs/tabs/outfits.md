# 👕 Outfits

**English** · [繁體中文](outfits_zh_TW.md) · [All pages](../README.md)

Viewers redeem a reward, you pick the outfit: your avatar's state in veadotube mini (2.1 or newer).

## Set up once

1. In veadotube mini → **program settings**, turn on the **WebSocket server**.
2. Open **👕 Outfits**: your veadotube windows show with a picture of each state. Click one to wear it.
3. Press **☆** on your usual outfit: it becomes the default, and its window the one redemptions change. With the
   same avatar in two windows, press ☆ in the right one, once after opening veadotube.
4. In [🎟️ Rewards](rewards.md), set the rewards that change the outfit to **👕 Outfit**.
5. Back here, under **Redemptions**, set **Back to the default outfit after** N minutes (0 = it stays until the
   next redemption).

## During a stream

When one is redeemed, a card with your outfits pops up on any tab: click the one to wear. Rewards the app manages
are then marked done, or **↩ Refund**; others stay in Twitch's own queue.

## When something goes wrong

- **No windows show:** veadotube's WebSocket server is off, or veadotube isn't running.
- **The wrong window changes:** press ☆ in the right window again (needed once after each veadotube start when the
  same avatar is open twice).
