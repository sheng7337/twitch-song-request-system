Leihu Singsing

Imports queued songs from the sheet's YouTube link and renders their key ahead of time. Uses the GPU during stream.

Leihu then runs without its own window and shows only in this tab. It closes when the song queue closes.

Leihu's sound as its own OBS source

Get your song list ready in Leihu

Leihu Singsing makes a backing track and timed lyrics from each song's YouTube video. Do these two steps once (and again after adding songs to the sheet), so requested songs are ready to sing right away.

① Find a YouTube link for each song

On that page press “Search songs without a link”: it finds the right video for each song that has no link yet, lets you check the picks, and saves them in the sheet's YTLink column. Songs that already have a link are left as they are.

🔗 Find YouTube links
② Prepare the songs in Leihu

Downloads each linked song Leihu doesn't have yet and makes its backing track and lyrics. Songs without a link are skipped, so do step ① first. It takes a few minutes per song and uses the GPU, so it's best started when you're not streaming.

Safe to leave running: one song at a time and only while Leihu is idle, so requested songs go first. If YouTube starts limiting downloads it stops by itself; press the button again a few hours later to continue.
