Finds, for each song in your sheet without a YouTube link, the video whose recording matches the lyrics Leihu Singsing can find — so its backing track and timed lyrics line up. Nothing is written to your sheet until you accept a video and press Save to sheet. Links go into a YTLink column, which is added to a tab the first time you save links there; filled cells are never changed. Leihu Singsing must be running.
